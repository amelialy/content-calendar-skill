# Stages and leaks

Use this to diagnose a business before planning content. Decide the **stage** first, then the **main leak**. When they disagree, plan around the leak.

## Business stages

| Stage | How to recognise it | What content must do |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-launch** | No paying customers yet. Building the product, service or space. | Get known before opening. Build a waitlist or early-interest list. Show the "why" behind the business. |
| **Early traction** | First customers, mostly friends, referrals or walk-ins. Few people outside that circle know them. | Get found by strangers and earn trust fast. Prove it's real and good. |
| **Growth** | Steady sales and a working offer. Wants more customers or bigger orders. | Turn attention into enquiries and enquiries into sales. Scale what already works. |
| **Established** | Known locally or in a niche, with a loyal base. Growth has slowed. | Bring customers back, get referrals, raise spend per customer and stay top of mind. |

Startups often sit at Pre-launch or Early traction even with funding. Judge the stage by customers, not by money.

## Funnel leaks

Find where people drop off. Use whatever rough numbers the user has.

| Leak | Symptoms the user describes | Funnel stage to fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Not seen** | Low views or reach. "Nobody sees my posts." Followers mostly friends and family. | Awareness |
| **Not trusted** | Decent views but few profile visits, follows or DMs. "People watch but don't do anything." | Consideration |
| **Not buying** | DMs or "how much?" enquiries arrive, but few become customers. Lots of "I'll think about it" and ghosting after price. | Conversion |
| **Not returning** | Customers buy once and disappear. Few reviews or referrals. Every sale is a new customer. | Retention |

### Rough benchmarks for SMEs (directional only)

- Enquiry-to-customer below about **20–30%** for a service business usually means **Not buying**.
- Under about **1 enquiry per 1,000 views** usually means **Not trusted**.
- Repeat rate under about **20–30%** for a repeat-purchase business (F&B, beauty, fitness, clinics) usually means **Not returning**.

Say these are rough guides, not facts. If the user's numbers are borderline, ask one follow-up question rather than guess.

## Writing the diagnosis

Keep it to 2–3 sentences in plain language: stage, leak, and what this period's content will lean on as a result. Then give one goal as the box's last line.

### Sizing the goal

The target must be something that can move **within the period**. If the real measure is slow (a 6-month return rate, say), pick a number that moves sooner and leads to it.

Size it from the user's own numbers. As a rule of thumb, aim for about **1.5–2× their current rate** over 4 weeks. Start from a small base when they have no numbers yet.

| Leak | Measure | Example from their numbers |
|---|---|---|
| Not seen | Average views per post, waitlist sign-ups | 200 views a post → aim for 300–400. No waitlist yet → 50–100 sign-ups from strangers in 4–6 weeks for a local SME. |
| Not trusted | Enquiries per week | 4 a week → aim for 6–8 |
| Not buying | Enquiry-to-customer rate | 2 in 10 → aim for 3 in 10 |
| Not returning | Past customers rebooked this period | 100 customers last half-year → 15–25 rebooked this month |

Say the target is a starting estimate the user can change.

## Beyond posts: fixes outside content

Suggest 1–3 of these in the hand-off. Content alone rarely closes a leak.

- **Not seen:** collaborate with a nearby business or creator, get listed on Google Maps or Business Profile, invite friends to share the launch post, and put a small budget on boosting the best Awareness post.
- **Not trusted:** pin 3 posts (story, proof, how it works), tidy the bio and its link, add a "highlights" FAQ and ask early customers for Google reviews.
- **Not buying:** write a WhatsApp reply template for price enquiries (price, what's included, next step), follow up "I'll think about it" leads within 48 hours, and make the booking link one tap.
- **Not returning:** send a WhatsApp or SMS recall when a customer is due, ask for a review after every visit, add a rebooking prompt at checkout and start a simple referral offer.
