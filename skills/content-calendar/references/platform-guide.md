# Platform guide

What each platform is good for, so posts go where they work best. **This is a starting point, not the latest truth.** Platforms change their feeds and features often, so run a quick search for recent changes when you can, and trust a newer, reliable source over this file.

Internal labels: "Get found" = Awareness, "Build trust" = Consideration, "Get them to buy" = Conversion, "Bring them back" = Retention.

## Quick picker: where to start

| If the business… | Start with | Why (say it plainly) |
|---|---|---|
| Is local and visual (café, salon, clinic, studio, retail) | Instagram + Facebook, plus a Google Business Profile | People check Instagram before visiting. Facebook still reaches older and family customers in Malaysia. |
| Needs to reach many new people fast, or sells products | TikTok | New accounts can reach people who don't follow them, and TikTok Shop links content to buying. |
| Sells to businesses or professionals (B2B, consulting, hiring) | LinkedIn | Decision-makers read it, and expertise posts travel. |
| Has ideas, opinions or expertise to share in writing | Threads | Text-first conversations. Replies build relationships quickly. |
| Sells to Chinese-speaking Malaysians or travellers (beauty, F&B, travel, lifestyle) | Xiaohongshu (RED) | Used as a search engine for reviews and recommendations |
| Has explainers, tutorials or longer stories | YouTube (Shorts to be found, long videos to build trust) | Videos keep getting found through search for months. |
| Already has customers to keep in touch with | WhatsApp channel or a newsletter | Goes straight to people who already know you |

**One platform done well beats three done badly.** For 2–3 posts a week, recommend one main platform and at most one where the same posts get reshared.

## By platform

### Instagram
- **Best for:** Build trust and Get them to buy. People check your profile, highlights and DMs before buying. Reels can help you get found.
- **Formats:** short video (Reels) to get found; carousels (swipe posts) to teach and save; stories for behind the scenes, polls and offers; highlights as a permanent FAQ.
- **What works:** saves and shares (DM sends) signal value. A clear first frame and on-screen text, since many people watch without sound. The profile works like a shop window.
- **Malaysia note:** "PM for price" and WhatsApp links in the bio are normal. Make the next step one tap.

### Facebook
- **Best for:** Bring them back, and reaching older and family audiences. Community groups and local buy-and-sell groups.
- **Formats:** short video (reshared Reels); photo posts; posts in local groups (follow group rules); events.
- **What works:** practical, local, family-friendly posts. Shares into groups and chats.
- **Malaysia note:** still strong for audiences aged 35+, parents and in smaller towns. Cross-post from Instagram with little extra work.

### TikTok
- **Best for:** Get found. It shows posts to people who don't follow you more than most platforms do.
- **Formats:** short video, from 15–60 seconds up to a few minutes for tutorials; photo slideshows; lives (strong for selling); TikTok Shop for products.
- **What works:** a strong first 1–2 seconds, a real face, a real place and a real process. Trends adapted to the business, not copied. Search-friendly words in captions and on screen, because many people search TikTok like Google.
- **Malaysia note:** a large, young, mobile audience. Bahasa Malaysia, Manglish or Chinese captions can widen reach.

### Threads
- **Best for:** Build trust through conversation, and Get found through replies and shared interests.
- **Formats:** text post (one strong idea); connected text posts (a short series for a method or list); a photo or short video with text; polls.
- **What works:** opinions and real stories in a natural voice. Replying to others is often as important as posting. Light, relatable posts on everyday topics (coffee, work life) bring new people. Links in the first reply rather than in the post.
- **Limits:** 500 characters per post. Use connected posts for anything longer.
- **Note:** better for ideas and conversation than for hard selling.

### LinkedIn
- **Best for:** Build trust with professionals and businesses. Get found through expertise.
- **Formats:** text post with a strong first line; document or carousel posts; short video; articles or newsletters for depth.
- **What works:** specific lessons from real work, numbers you're allowed to share, and comments from people in the industry. Post on weekday mornings or lunchtimes.

### Xiaohongshu (RED)
- **Best for:** Get found through search, and Build trust through reviews and guides.
- **Formats:** photo notes (cover image plus several photos) with detailed text; short video.
- **What works:** an honest, detailed experience written like a friend's review; clear cover titles; searchable keywords (place names, product types).
- **Malaysia note:** strong among Chinese-speaking Malaysians and visitors from China. Write in Simplified Chinese for reach.

### YouTube
- **Best for:** Shorts help you get found. Long videos build trust and are found through search for a long time.
- **Formats:** Shorts (under 3 minutes); tutorials; reviews; "how we do it" videos.
- **What works:** titles that match what people search; useful, specific answers.

### WhatsApp channel / newsletter
- **Best for:** Bring them back and Get them to buy, with people who already know you.
- **Formats:** short updates, offers, early access, tips.
- **What works:** a steady, predictable rhythm (e.g. weekly), and real value, not just promotions. Don't overdo it; 1–2 a week is plenty for a channel.

## Matching goals to formats (any platform)

| Goal | Formats that usually work |
|---|---|
| Get found | Short video, bold-opinion text posts, shareable tips, trends adapted to the business |
| Build trust | Carousels or connected posts that teach, behind-the-scenes stories, founder stories, lives |
| Get them to buy | Posts that answer a doubt, before-and-afters, offer posts with a clear next step, story polls leading to DMs |
| Bring them back | Stories, WhatsApp channel or newsletter updates, customer spotlights, members-first offers |

## Posting times

Treat "best time" charts as a rough guide. In Malaysia, people are often online in the morning commute (7–9am), at lunch (12–2pm) and in the evening (8–11pm). The audience's own data matters more. If the user has insights from the platform's dashboard, use their most active hours. And a post does best when the owner can reply to comments in the first half hour after posting.
