# Funnel mix

Decide what share of posts goes to each funnel stage, turn that into whole post counts, then decide how to measure each post.

## Funnel stages

- **Awareness:** reaches people who don't know you yet. Broad, useful, shareable.
- **Consideration:** makes people who've seen you trust you. Shows who you are and how you work.
- **Conversion:** helps people who are interested decide to buy. Proof, objections, clear offers, waitlists.
- **Retention:** keeps customers coming back and recommending you.

## 1. Base mix by stage

| Stage | Awareness | Consideration | Conversion | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-launch | 60% | 30% | 10% (waitlist / pre-order) | 0% |
| Early traction | 45% | 30% | 20% | 5% |
| Growth | 35% | 25% | 30% | 10% |
| Established | 25% | 20% | 25% | 30% |

## 2. Leak adjustment

Move **15 percentage points** to the stage that fixes the leak. Take them from the "take from" stages in order. If a stage would drop below its floor, take the rest from the next stage in the list.

| Leak | Add to | Take from, in this order |
|---|---|---|
| Not seen | Awareness | Consideration, then Conversion |
| Not trusted | Consideration | Awareness, then Conversion |
| Not buying | Conversion | Awareness, then Consideration |
| Not returning | Retention | Awareness, then Consideration |

**Floors:**
- Conversion never goes below **5%** while there's anything to sell or sign up for (including a waitlist).
- Awareness never goes below **10%**.
- Retention may be 0% only before the first customer.

**Worked examples:**
- Pre-launch + Not seen → 75 / 15 / 10 / 0
- Growth + Not buying → 20 / 25 / 45 / 10
- Established + Not returning → 10 / 20 / 25 / 45

## 3. Turn percentages into post counts

1. Multiply each percentage by the total number of posts.
2. Give each stage the whole-number part.
3. Hand out the posts that are left one at a time, to the stages with the largest remainders. **On a tie, the leak's stage wins**; then Conversion, then Consideration.
4. Check the counts add up to the total.

Example: 12 posts at 10 / 20 / 25 / 45 → 1.2 / 2.4 / 3.0 / 5.4 → whole parts 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 = 11. The last post goes to the 0.4 tie, and the leak (Retention) wins → **1 / 2 / 3 / 6**.

If the period crosses a launch, work out the counts separately for the weeks before and after launch.

**Count floor:** once a business has customers, a part of the plan that runs 4 weeks or longer gets at least 1 Retention post. If rounding gives 0, take 1 from the largest stage. Shorter parts may round Retention to 0.

## One success metric per post

| Funnel stage | Measure this | Not this |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Shares, saves, reach from non-followers | Sales |
| Consideration | Profile visits, follows, replies, story replies | Likes alone |
| Conversion | DMs, WhatsApp enquiries, link clicks, waitlist sign-ups, bookings | Reach |
| Retention | Repeat bookings, reviews, referrals, customers tagging you | New followers |

## What to track for the review

Pick the 1–2 numbers that show the main leak closing:

- Not seen → average reach per post, new followers per week (and waitlist size if pre-launch)
- Not trusted → profile visits per week, enquiries per 1,000 views
- Not buying → enquiry-to-customer rate
- Not returning → returning customers this period, new reviews
