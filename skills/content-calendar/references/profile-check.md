# Profile check

How to read someone's public profile and turn it into planning decisions. Keep it read-only, and look only at public information.

## How to open it

**Which tool sees what:**
- **A real browser tool** (one that opens and shows the page, then lets you scroll) usually shows posts and their numbers on public profiles. Use it first if you have one.
- **A plain web fetch, or downloading the page,** usually gets only the name, bio and sometimes follower count. Posts load later in the browser, so a fetch rarely sees them. Downloading a public page without logging in is fine.

| Platform | Public URL (try this first) | Without login, you can usually see |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | `instagram.com/<handle>` | Browser: the first few posts, then a login wall. Fetch: bio only. |
| TikTok | `tiktok.com/@<handle>` | Browser: videos with view counts. Fetch: bio, sometimes counts. |
| Threads | `threads.com/@<handle>` | Browser: some posts. Fetch: name, bio and follower count only. |
| Facebook page | `facebook.com/<page>` | Partly. Login prompts are common. |
| LinkedIn | `linkedin.com/in/<name>` or `/company/<name>` | Headline only |
| YouTube | `youtube.com/@<handle>` | Videos with view counts, even by fetch |
| Xiaohongshu (RED) | `xiaohongshu.com/user/profile/<id>` | Very little |

**If no posts are visible, treat it as "couldn't see the posts"**, whether it was a login wall, an empty page or a page that loads its posts later.

If you hit a login wall, stop. Never log in or use the user's sessions to get past it. Ask for screenshots instead.

## What to check (up to 10 minutes' worth)

**1. The profile itself: would a stranger follow or message?**
- **Bio:** does it say who they help, with what, and what to do next? Is there a link (booking, WhatsApp, newsletter, shop)?
- **Name and photo:** clear and recognisable? Does the name include a searchable word (e.g. "Ana | Pilates PJ")?
- **Pinned posts and highlights:** do they show who they are, proof and how to buy?

**2. The last 9–12 posts**
- **Rhythm:** how often do they post, and are there long gaps?
- **Mix of formats:** video, carousel, photo, text?
- **Topics:** what do they mostly talk about? Is it what customers care about, or only what they sell?
- **First lines:** do they make you want to read on?
- **Asks:** do posts invite a reply, save, share or message? Or do they just end?
- **Voice:** casual or formal? Language (English, BM, Chinese, Manglish)? Emojis?

**3. What performed: compare with their own usual, not other accounts**
- Find their usual number: the middle value of views, likes or comments across recent posts.
- Note posts at **3× or more** their usual. What do they share: topic, format, first line, face, place?
- Note posts that did well under their usual. Look for what they share, and one tweak worth trying next time.
- Read the comments on the best post. Who replied, and what did they say?

## Turning it into decisions

| What you see | What it means | What to do in the plan |
|---|---|---|
| Good views, few follows; unclear bio | People come but don't stay | Fix the bio and pins first. Plan more "Build trust" posts. |
| One topic or format far above usual | People want more of it | Give it more slots and reuse its style for "Get found" posts |
| Mostly product photos and promotions | Followers mostly see offers | Keep the product posts, and add stories, tips and behind the scenes around them |
| Long gaps, then bursts | Hard to keep up | Plan a rhythm they can keep. Suggest writing several in one sitting. |
| Posts never ask for anything | No next step | Give every post a clear ask |
| Comments asking price or details go unanswered | Lost sales | "Also worth doing": reply within a day, and use a ready reply for price questions |
| Very few posts or a new account | Nothing to learn from yet | Say so, and plan from their answers and the platform guide |

## How to report it to the user

3–5 bullets in plain words, starting with what's working. Never tell them to stop posting something they enjoy; say what it does for them and what to try. For example:

- **Working:** your coffee post got about 10× your usual views. People like real everyday moments from you.
- **Try next:** your opinion posts about "marketing systems" get 1–2 likes so far. Adding one real example from your week could get more people replying.
- **Profile:** your bio doesn't say who you help or link to your newsletter. Many people visit but few follow.
- **Quick fix:** rewrite the bio in one line, add your newsletter link and pin your best post.

Only state numbers you actually saw, and only ones that look right. Leave out a number that looks odd.

**If you only saw part of the profile** (e.g. the bio but no posts), give fewer bullets rather than padding. Even one is fine. Say plainly what you couldn't see, and in the same message ask for a screenshot of the profile and last 9 posts, or of their insights. If they'd rather not, plan from their answers.
