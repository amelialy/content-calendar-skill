# Story formats

Every post is a small story: **Hook → Tension → Turn → Lesson/Proof → CTA.** Choose a format suited to the post's funnel stage, then fill in the beats in one line.

Rotate formats so no two posts in a row use the same one.

**The SME examples below are illustrations only.** Never copy their specifics (numbers, events, results) into a plan. Use the user's real facts, or a `[placeholder]`.

## Awareness: stop the scroll and be worth sharing

| Format | Beats | SME example |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook–Story–Lesson** | Surprising line → short moment → what it taught you | "We lost 3 bookings in one day because of one sentence on our menu." |
| **Myth vs truth** | Common belief → why it's wrong → what's true | "Pilates is only for flexible people. Actually, the opposite." |
| **"I used to think…"** | Old belief → what changed it → new belief | Founder admits a mistake customers also make |
| **Quick win** | Pain → 3 steps → result they can feel today | 5-minute desk stretch, 1-minute skincare fix |
| **Hot take** | Bold opinion → reason → invite disagreement | "Stop running giveaways for followers." |

## Consideration: build trust and show who you are

| Format | Beats | SME example |
|---|---|---|
| **Founder story** | Why you started → the hard moment → why you keep going | The day the clinic nearly closed |
| **Behind the scenes** | What customers never see → the care in it → why it matters | Sunday-night studio prep, sourcing trips |
| **Problem–Agitate–Solve** | Name the pain → make it real → your way of solving it | "Back pain from sitting? Here's what's actually happening." |
| **Day in the life (of a customer)** | Their before → their visit → their after | A first-timer's walk through your door |
| **How we do it** | Your process step by step → the standard you hold | 7 steps every facial goes through |

## Conversion: help them decide

| Format | Beats | SME example |
|---|---|---|
| **Objection buster** | The doubt in their words → honest answer → proof → CTA | "Is RM 180 worth it for one session?" |
| **Before / After / Bridge** | Life before → life after → your offer is the bridge | Messy books before, clean dashboard after |
| **Customer case story** | Who they were → the problem → what changed (real, with permission) → CTA | A member's first month |
| **FAQ answer** | One question customers WhatsApp you → clear answer → CTA | "Do I need experience?" |
| **Offer post** | Who it's for → what they get → why now (honest urgency) → how to claim | Intro pack, festive bundle |
| **Comparison** | Option A vs B vs you → who each suits → why you suit them | DIY vs salon vs home-visit |

## Campaign arc for launches and offers

Spread the arc over 1–2 weeks. Each step has a fixed funnel stage and counts against that stage's post budget.

| Arc step | Funnel stage | Good formats | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tease** | Awareness | Hook–Story–Lesson, Hot take | Date plus a glimpse. Don't reveal everything yet. |
| **Reveal** | Consideration | How we do it, Founder story | What it is, who it's for, the price |
| **Proof** | Consideration | Day in the life, Behind the scenes | Real proof only. **Before launch, with no customers, quote feedback from a real tasting or trial session (with permission), or show the process instead.** |
| **Offer** | Conversion | Offer post, Objection buster | Real offer, real price |
| **Last call** | Conversion | Offer post | Only with a real cut-off date. Not next to the Offer post. |
| **Recap** | Retention after the first customers, otherwise Consideration | Milestone with the community | Thank people, and use real numbers only |

If the budget is too small, drop steps in this order: Recap, Proof, Tease.

## Retention: bring them back and get them talking

| Format | Beats | SME example |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer spotlight** | Real customer → their story → thank-you (with permission) | Member of the month |
| **"You asked, we built"** | What customers requested → what you changed → invite more ideas | New evening class slot |
| **Insider / members-first** | Something only customers get → how to use it | Early booking for festive slots |
| **Milestone with the community** | Number or moment → credit to customers → what's next | "Our 1,000th booking was…" |
| **Care tip** | How to get more from what they bought → reminder to rebook | Aftercare guide, maintenance schedule |
