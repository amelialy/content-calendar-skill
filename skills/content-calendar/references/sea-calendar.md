# Southeast Asia marketing calendar

Use this to plan around dates that change what customers do. **Lunar and Islamic holidays move every year, so always confirm exact dates for the plan's year** (search, or ask the user) before putting them in the calendar. For markets outside this list, check the user's local holidays and shopping events.

## Shopping events (every year, fixed dates)

| Date | Event | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Payday, around the 25th–1st | Monthly payday window | Put Conversion and Offer posts here. Avoid launching offers just before payday. |
| 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12 | Double-date sales (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop) | People are primed to buy. Run an offer only if it's genuine; otherwise run Awareness posts, because ads cost more that week. |
| Last Friday of Nov, then the Monday after | Black Friday / Cyber Monday | Mainly urban and online audiences |
| 14 Feb | Valentine's Day | F&B, gifting, beauty, couples experiences |
| Year-end, Dec | Year-end school holidays, bonus season | Family offers, gift cards, "new year, new you" set-up |

## Festive seasons (dates move, so confirm each year)

| Occasion | Usually | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Chinese New Year | Jan–Feb | Pre-CNY rush (beauty, hair, gifting, F&B catering). Many businesses close for several days. Schedule a "closed" post and a "we're back" post. |
| Ramadan | 29–30 days before Hari Raya | Iftar and buka puasa offers. Shift posting times to evenings and after Tarawih. Be respectful; avoid food-heavy posts at midday for Muslim audiences. |
| Hari Raya Aidilfitri | After Ramadan | Pre-Raya rush for outfits, beauty, hampers and home. A quiet week after, as people balik kampung. |
| Hari Raya Haji | About 2 months after Aidilfitri | Smaller occasion; a greeting post |
| Deepavali | Oct–Nov | Beauty, outfits, sweets, home décor. Greeting and gifting posts. |
| Christmas | 25 Dec | Gifting, parties, year-end gatherings |
| Mid-Autumn Festival | Sep–Oct | Mooncakes, gifting, family gatherings |

| Thaipusam | Jan–Feb | Public holiday in several Malaysian states, including Selangor, KL and Penang. Greeting post, no hard sell. |
| Wesak | Apr–Jun | A greeting post |
| Awal Muharram, Maulidur Rasul, Israk Mikraj, Nuzul Al-Quran | Move each year | Islamic observances; some are state holidays. A respectful greeting post or none, and no hard sell. |

## Malaysia: other dates that matter

- **State holidays:** each state has its own, e.g. the Sultan of Selangor's Birthday (11 Dec) and Federal Territory Day (1 Feb). Check the user's state.
- **Agong's Birthday:** a national holiday in early June.
- **School year start:** usually early January, a good hook for families and back-to-routine posts. Check the Ministry of Education calendar for the year.
- **Year-end leave (mid-Dec to early Jan):** offices empty out. B2B and office-focused products get less attention, so plan Awareness posts or a back-to-work hook for the first week of January.

## When sources disagree

Lunar and Islamic dates sometimes differ between sources, or shift once the official announcement comes. Don't put a key post (Offer, Last call, launch) on a disputed date. Tell the user which date to double-check.

## National days

- **Malaysia:** Merdeka (31 Aug), Malaysia Day (16 Sep)
- **Singapore:** National Day (9 Aug)
- **Indonesia:** Independence Day (17 Aug)
- **Philippines:** Independence Day (12 Jun)

These work well for local-pride and "made in" posts. Avoid hard-selling on the day itself.

## School holidays

School holidays change parents' schedules and family spending. Check the current year's school calendar for the user's country, as it changes each year.
