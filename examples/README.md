# Example: Studio Lumi Pilates (fictional)

**The brief given to Claude:**

- **Sells:** pilates classes to office workers in Petaling Jaya, Malaysia
- **Posts on:** Instagram feed + Stories
- **Can post:** 3 times a week (Mon / Wed / Fri)
- **Period:** 4 weeks from 23 Nov 2026
- **Happening:** December Intro Pack launch, 12.12 sale weekend
- **Goal:** trial class bookings through WhatsApp

**Weekly themes Claude picked:**

1. Pilates is for beginners too
2. December Intro Pack launch
3. 12.12 and year-end desk season
4. Keep moving through the school holidays

**Files:**

- [`content-calendar.csv`](content-calendar.csv): 12 posts. Opens in Google Sheets, Notion or Excel.
- [`content-calendar.ics`](content-calendar.ics): 12 all-day events. Import it into a new calendar to try it.

"Prove" posts are prompts for real member stories and real studio numbers. The skill never invents them.
