# Example: Seri Smile Dental (fictional)

An **established** business with a **retention** problem.

## The brief

- **What they offer:** a family dental clinic in Subang Jaya, Malaysia, open for 8 years. Check-up + scaling RM 150, whitening RM 1,200, braces from RM 5,000.
- **Numbers:** 15,000 followers, 3,000–5,000 reach a post, about 25 WhatsApp enquiries a week, 60% of which book. But only about **15% of patients come back** for their 6-month check-up.
- **Customer questions:** "Does scaling hurt?" "Is my check-up covered by my panel or insurance?" "How much is whitening and is it safe?"
- **Plan:** Instagram + Facebook (cross-posted), 3 posts a week, 4 weeks from 4 Jan 2027

## The diagnosis

> **Diagnosis:** You're at *Established*. Your main leak is *Not returning*: only about 15% of patients come back for their 6-month check-up. People do find you (about 2 enquiries per 1,000 views) and do book (6 in 10). So this month leans on bringing past patients back, plus straight answers to your three most-asked questions.
> **Goal:** Get 30 past patients to book their overdue check-up by 31 January.

## The mix

The Established base mix (25 / 20 / 25 / 30) is moved 15 points toward Retention, giving **10 / 20 / 25 / 45**. Over 12 posts, that's **1 Awareness, 2 Consideration, 3 Conversion and 6 Retention**.

## Dates it planned around

- **School reopening (4 Jan):** opens the month with a kids' check-up post.
- **Thaipusam (22 Jan):** a soft care-tip post with no hard sell.
- **Payday (end of January):** the whitening comparison post goes here.
- **The pre-CNY rush:** the month ends with a members-first recall post.

## Files

- [`content-calendar.csv`](content-calendar.csv): 12 posts, each with funnel stage, pillar, story format, hook, story beats, CTA and success metric
- [`content-calendar.ics`](content-calendar.ics): 12 all-day events

Customer stories are written as prompts ("with permission"), never invented.
